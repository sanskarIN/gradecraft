# Code of Conduct

GradeCraft follows a simple contributor standard: be respectful, constructive, inclusive, and focused on improving the project.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized harassment, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and restrict participation when necessary.

Report conduct concerns privately to `supportramsandesh@gmail.com`. Do not use public issues for sensitive reports.
